Swap between two crests in combat, rather than being stuck with only one. Unlike other crest swapping mods, this one is meant to feel like a FEATURE, rather than a dev tool.

Things to note:
* Keybind is a remappable Unity KeyCode. (controller works, but google a unity KeyCode diagram for your preferred controller)
* Costs 1 silk notch
* Bind buffs (such as Reaper Mode) transfer to and from your alt slot.
* Independent tool slots: You can have a whole set of tools in your main slot, and a whole set in your alt slot. (Vesticrest slots are shared.)
